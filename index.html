About Me

Designing & Verifying Modern Silicon

I'm a Digital IC Design & Verification Engineer based in Giza, Egypt, with a strong foundation in RTL design, SystemVerilog, and UVM-based verification.

My graduation project — an NB-IoT LTE Downlink Low-Power Transmitter — took me through the complete ASIC flow: from MATLAB modeling and RTL implementation to synthesis, place-and-route, STA, and sign-off using Synopsys and Cadence tools.

I'm passionate about building robust digital blocks and crafting verification environments that catch bugs before silicon ever sees the fab.
